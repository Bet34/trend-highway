Crypto · withdrawals target Sun Nov 1

1. Binance says 90% of the $6.3B hack is now covered; withdrawals still paused

Binance said Saturday it has restored backing for about 90% of the roughly $6.3 billion in token assets stolen from its hot wallets. CNBC stresses that this means Binance replaced them with its own money, not that it recovered 90% from the attacker. BTC, ETH, USDT, BNB, SOL, USDC, XRP, USD1, RLUSD and ZEC are "fully backed 100%"; the remaining 10% is smaller, less liquid tokens it is still buying (CNBC, BBC). The money came from its SAFU emergency fund, part of its futures insurance fund and its corporate treasury. Binance took a fresh proof-of-reserves snapshot at 00:00 UTC Oct 31; the full zero-knowledge proof that lets users check their own balances should be ready by about Nov 3 (Business Today). Withdrawals are still off and remain "on track" for Sunday, Nov. 1, subject to security checks. Friday's US spot Bitcoin ETFs saw $643.3 million of net outflows, the most since June (crypto.news). Prices are firmer this afternoon: Bitcoin ~$77,536 (+1.2% in 24h), ETH ~$2,254 (+1.1%), BNB ~$707 (+1.5%) at ~3:03 PM ET (CoinGecko).
