Crypto · confirmed Oct 30

1. Binance: $6.3B stolen, North Korea blamed

Reuters (via Yahoo) and AP report hackers took about $6.3 billion from Binance, possibly the largest financial heist ever; most of it moved in about 20 minutes starting late Oct 29. TRM Labs (~$6.36B across ten blockchains) and Chainalysis (~$6.4B) both attribute it with high confidence to North Korea's TraderTraitor unit. About $3 billion has been frozen (TRM counts ~$2.67B in stablecoins frozen by Tether, Circle, Ripple and World Liberty, plus Arbitrum freezes), Binance says it reserved ~$6.34B of company funds to cover all losses, and withdrawals are expected back within about 48 hours. Reuters says the FBI and Secret Service are working with Binance. Bitcoin was ~$77,270 (-3.5% in 24h), ETH ~$2,229 (-4.1%) and BNB ~$699 (-5.5%) at ~9:11 AM ET (CoinGecko).
